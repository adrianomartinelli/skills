## What it does

You type `/mobile`, or tell the agent you are on your phone. From then on, every reply is one to three chat lines plus a round of tappable cards in the question UI. There are four kinds of card: **assumptions** to confirm or revise, **decisions** to keep or swap for an alternative, **issues** to track, ignore or explain, and **questions** with options, the recommended one first. Every card has a **More context** option, and "Other" lets you answer in your own words.

## When to reach for it

When you follow a session from your phone and long prose replies are hard to read and answer. The agent also reaches for it on its own when you say you are on mobile or away from the desk.

## Common questions

**Does it work with grilling?** Yes. Grilling still decides what to ask; `mobile` decides how. Each grilling round becomes a round of cards, and open questions stay open through "Other".

**How do I turn it off?** Say "desk mode" or "stop mobile".

**What if there are more than four cards?** The agent makes several question calls in one reply, most important first.

**Does the agent stop to ask before it starts?** On a new task, yes: the first round holds only the assumptions the work rests on, and nothing that depends on one runs until you confirm or revise it.

## It's working if

- You can answer every reply without typing.
- No reply sends you to a file, an HTML page or an artifact to read.
- "More context" gives a short explanation and asks the same card again.
- "Track as issue" leaves an issue number in the next reply.
